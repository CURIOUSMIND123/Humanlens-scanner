Teach Tech3
The real situation

Our schools face three honest challenges

  • Distance and internet. Many schools are remote. Anything that needs the internet simply does not work there.
  • Learning is hard from words alone. Children understand and remember far more when they can see and touch a lesson.
  • The department cannot see everything. With so many schools, it is hard to know what is working and where help is needed.
Teach Tech4
Our answer

One system. Three simple parts.

FOR THE CHILD

The Board

Lessons on the smart board that work offline, in the child's own language.

FOR THE SCHOOL

The School ERP

One easy tool for attendance, funds, bills and meals — less paperwork.

FOR THE DEPARTMENT

The Command Centre

One screen to see every school — what is working, where to help.

And the State owns the whole system. It is not rented.

Teach Tech5
Part 1 · For the child

The Board

  • 3D lessons and science experiments the child can touch and explore, not just watch.
  • Works with no internet — everything is stored on the board or a pen drive.
  • In English, Hindi and the local language, with a script that helps the teacher.
  • A child who sees the Earth from space, or builds a real circuit, remembers it for life.
Teach Tech6
Part 2 · For the school

The School ERP

  • One place for staff and student attendance, funds, bills and meals.
  • Teachers spend less time on registers and more time teaching.
  • Bills are uploaded directly — clean records, no lost paperwork.
  • It works offline too, and syncs when the school gets internet for a while.
Teach Tech7
Part 3 · For the department

The Command Centre

  • One screen for every school — attendance, funds, meals and equipment.
  • It quietly points out schools that need a closer look, so officers can help early.
  • It never accuses anyone — it just hands your office the facts, on its own.
  • The data arrives whenever a school connects; no one has to chase reports.

This is only the beginning

Everything you saw was built in about 30 minutes

  • If this much is possible in half an hour, imagine a full library built over a year.
  • We can create our own complete content library — class by class, chapter by chapter.

The heart of it

How this helps the student

  • They understand better — because they see and do, not just listen.
  • They learn in their own language, so no child is left behind.
  • Their teacher is present and supported, with ready lessons every day.
  • And the whole system is honest — money and attendance are tracked, so help reaches the classroom.

How we will deliver

Start small, prove it, then grow

  • Step 1 — a small pilot. A handful of schools in one term, so you see real results before any big spend.
  • We handle the software and content. Teach Tech builds, installs and runs the platform.
  • Adidev handles the ground work. Electrical, installation, the attendance devices and local service.
  • Then we scale on evidence — the same system, already owned by the State, extends to more schools at low cost.
